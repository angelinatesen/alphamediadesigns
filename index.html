About Me

Design Is a Passion

Starting with an idea and making it visible for the naked eye to appreciate is pure joy. I have been designing since I was old enough to rearrange my mom's living room.

It has been quite satisfying over the years to get to the finished product of a birthday invite, baby shower event, flower pot arrangement, a gift and its unique wrapping, or a menu layout.

I look forward to new endeavors with present, past, and new clients. It will be my privilege.

Angelina N. Tesen
Founder & Creative Director
